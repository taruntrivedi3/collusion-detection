# LLM double-auction null study: frozen before scan results

The upstream dataset is pinned at `2bb2a7e22860160d1fddb5d2e5cb460cc6ec6aff` in andyjliu/llm-agent-collusion. It contains 75 markets, five sellers and five buyers, 30 rounds. The existing parser is reused and its output must reconcile numeric decisions, trade arithmetic, evaluator identities and published economic totals. All new files and copied inputs live here. There are no paid calls or new LLM rollouts.

## Questions

Can within-state shuffling or whole-column circular shifts provide useful dependence flags with only 29 usable LLM action observations? How sensitive are flags to recorded state? What happens on a known no-interaction empirical-path benchmark, and when calibration transfers to different source markets? Can the scan identify strong copied-path dependence? These questions do not assume that the published no-message condition is truly noncolluding.

## Statistic and features

Use the subset scan only, with the existing price-level and quote-direction encodings, keeping both views bundled under each agent's transformations. Round one is excluded because direction needs a preceding action. Price levels are <=$90, ($90,$95], >$95, withdrawal, and JSON-null/no update. Direction distinguishes decrease, hold, increase, quote after no numeric submission, withdrawal and no update. Literal string "null" and JSON null remain distinct. No message classifiers, evaluator labels, TC/IPF or feature-bin tuning are used in the statistic. Scan all 26 groups of size at least two among five sellers, separately in two views; one maximum covers all 52 candidates. No growth or iterative removal. Degenerate edges/candidates with bank standard deviation <=1e-10 contribute zero, as in the existing auction prototype. Both normalizations use sample standard deviations (ddof=1). Each reference bank has 999 draws, with p=(1+number of reference maxima >= observed)/1000; near ties within relative 1e-10 count conservatively.

## Actual LLM trajectories

Analyze each market independently using source-style method-specific A/B banks. Compare no state, time thirds, five-round blocks, time third plus prior trade activity, a richer observed state, and exact round. Rich observed state contains time third, previous trade activity, previous seller level profile and each seller's visible best bid/ask bands. It is a richness diagnostic, not a sufficient-state claim; different within-round books can also make joint conditioning problematic. Exact round is a deliberate degeneracy diagnostic: only one observation is available at each time. Record state occupancy, within-state variation, candidate degeneracy, ranks and winning groups. Flags are surrogate dependence flags, not demonstrated collusion and not established 5% errors. The published evaluator and market outcomes remain descriptive comparison data.

For matched GPT-only conditions, compare each entire market's scalar maximum with the ten base markets' maxima (leave one out for base itself), separately for method and state. The minimum rank is 1/11 for a treatment or 1/10 for a base market. This cannot support a 5% rejection. Do not substitute thousands of synthetic resamples for independent actual-market controls.

## Known empirical no-interaction benchmark

Freeze the 50 full seller paths from the ten base markets as an empirical distribution. Independently draw one complete 29-round path with replacement for each of five artificial agents. Each path includes its paired direction/level views. Thus individual persistence, drift, flat quotes and action encodings are preserved. Conditional on the frozen pool, the five random complete paths are independent. Same-donor coincidences are allowed, as required for independent draws from a finite empirical distribution, and are represented in the reference distribution. These artificial groups did not interact with one another, but their source agents reacted to their original markets. Their joint public histories are not dynamically valid auction histories. This is a controlled empirical-path benchmark, not a new LLM market or a known honest live-market simulator.

Compare no state, time thirds, five-round blocks and exact round. A common fixed normalization is trained on 2,000 independent empirical-path groups, each independently shuffled within that state before fitting A. This avoids training the normalization to absorb the same temporal dependence being tested. Hold it fixed for all three nulls: within-state shuffle, circular shift, and fresh independent draws from the same empirical path distribution. The last is an exact model-based reference for this benchmark conditional on the fixed pool and independent normalization. It is not an oracle for actual interacting LLM markets. In parallel, score the same observed games and B draws with independent per-trajectory A banks for the two surrogate methods, to check source-style normalization.

Per state: 1,000 benchmark groups for optional empirical calibration, 1,000 separate benchmark evaluation groups, and 1,000 copied-path groups. Empirical calibration ranks a new raw p-value against the 1,000 calibration p-values: adjusted_p=(1+count(calibration_p<=new_p))/1001. It is conservative with ties. Report actual achieved size, not assumed equality to 5%. Pointwise Wilson intervals are conditional on the frozen pool/banks; 1,000 resamples do not provide 1,000 independent source-market observations. Separately apply Holm-adjusted one-sided binomial diagnostics of inflation within the prespecified benchmark family, with degenerate exact-round rows retained.

## Dependence stress test

For agents 0,1,2, choose one uniformly sampled donor path and copy it completely to all three. The other agents receive independent donor paths. Every individual still has exactly the same marginal full-path distribution as under the empirical benchmark. This is strong planted statistical dependence, not economically demonstrated collusion. Record detection, exact triple recovery, and including any innocent agent among all 1,000 groups. Some donor paths are constant within the chosen state; dependence statistics then contain little or no information, even for perfect copies. Include an analytical constant-$99 agreement example: constant actions have zero pairwise MI.

## Held-out source-market transfer

Use five fixed folds, each holding out two of the ten base markets (sorted by identifier). Fit normalization and draw reference paths from the other eight markets (40 paths); independently generate 500 evaluation groups from the held-out two markets (10 paths), for each state and fold. All generated agents remain independent conditional on their held-out pool. This probes misspecification/transfer of an empirical reference. It is not covered by the same-distribution reference guarantee. Report each fold and ranges; do not turn five folds into thousands of independent live-market controls. Surrogate source-style sensitivity is also retained.

## Interpretation and verification

Large rejection rates on independently sampled paths would show failure as a calibrated no-interaction detector for that empirical temporal process. Coarse states need not remove legitimate common time trends, so this is not a counterexample to permutation validity under satisfied exchangeability assumptions. Adding state may consume nearly all 29 observations, causing powerless/degenerate scans. A finite-sample guarantee for real LLM markets still needs correctly specified honest policies, valid conditional randomization, or enough matched independent honest market runs. Use the findings to establish what works and what does not; do not infer collusion from dependence or certification from the no-message label.

Verify MI and subset scores against the supplied auction implementation; preservation of bundled features, conditional counts and cyclic order; donor/clone construction; rank ties; sparse/constant degeneracy; strong generic triple recovery; frozen hashes; and replay of saved seeds. Preserve all observed donor IDs, original episodes, per-game p-values/winners, reference quantiles, configuration, source hashes, figures and results.
